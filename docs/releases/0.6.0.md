# Gridfees AUT 0.6.0

Beta — under active development. Use at your own risk; verify calculated prices
against the applicable regulations and your grid operator's published tariffs.

- English setup instructions and documentation, retaining official German grid
  area names, fee/tax terms, and legal references.
- Revised household Elektrizitätsabgabe help explains H0, HA, HF and natural-person
  eligibility for 2026, with links to ElAbgG and Stromkostenzuschussgesetz.
- Expanded SNAP label and help with the summer window and SNE-V 2018 links.
- New **Simulate WiNAP (Winter-Nieder-Arbeitspreis)** toggle, off by default:
  uses the selected tariff's 2026 SNAP price during nights starting 1 October
  through 31 March, 23:00–05:00 Europe/Vienna. This is a fictional scenario;
  the legal WiNAP first applies on 1 October 2027 and its price is not assumed.
- Current prices and the seven-day forecast use the same simulation setting.
  Attributes identify simulated prices; SNAP active remains a summer indicator.
- **Reconfigure** changes assumptions for an existing regular grid profile while
  preserving its entity IDs and history. EEG profiles retain automatic exemptions
  and their existing reduction; no WiNAP scenario is applied to allocated EEG energy.

## Update

Install **0.6.0** through HACS and restart Home Assistant. Do not delete or recreate
existing profiles. WiNAP simulation remains off until explicitly enabled through
the profile's **Reconfigure** menu under Settings → Devices & services → Gridfees AUT.

The published tariff dataset still ends on 31 December 2026. No 2027 tariffs are
extrapolated, including when simulation is enabled. Historical recorded prices
are not rewritten when the simulation setting changes.
